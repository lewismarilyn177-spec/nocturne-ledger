NOCTURNE LEDGER

a stealth affair in three acts

You are Wren, a night courier retrieving sealed ledgers from the Vaultine guildhalls. Cross each floor unseen: slip through shadow, hug cover, and vanish past the sentries. Guards see light cones, hear running and thrown lures.
